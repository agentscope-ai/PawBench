### Comparison of GISTEMP and gcag for 1880s–2010s
- The two sources agree closely, with minor differences within ±0.05°C for most decades.
- The largest discrepancy is in the 1880s, where GISTEMP reports an average anomaly 0.05°C higher than gcag.

### Summary
Warming has progressed steadily from the 1880s to the 2020s, with the rate of increase accelerating particularly after the 1970s. The 2010s saw the highest average anomaly, and the 2020s are on track to be even warmer, though only partial data is available. The 1880s had the most intra-decade variability, while the 1960s were the least variable. The largest single-decade warming jump was from the 1990s to the 2000s.
